Prank Ludo: Fool Your Friends ("the app") is an offline board game. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or device data. It does not require an account or sign-in, and it has no ads, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.

Internet access

The app works entirely offline and does not request permission to access the internet. All gameplay, including Prank Mode, happens locally on your device.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Third parties

Because no data is collected, no data is shared with or sold to any third party.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
